How the app works

Three steps to full project control — no installation required.

  1. Download & unzip Click ⬇️ Download App above — the zip is fetched from the latest GitHub release and saved to your Downloads folder. Unzip it anywhere (double-click in Finder).
  2. Open the app Double-click app.html — it opens in your browser and works instantly. No install, no dependencies. (You can also use 🚀 Open App locally above, which launches the copy on this machine.)
  3. Scan your projects Click Scan folder and pick ~/Projects — every subfolder becomes a project. Or click + New to create one with a real folder on disk. Tick tasks, watch the dashboard fill up.
  4. Connect your database Click 💾 Connect DB (same folder is fine) — the app creates jrispace-pm.db and saves every change to it automatically. Clear your browser cache all you want: your projects live on disk, not in the browser.

Getting the latest update

The download button always fetches the latest release from GitHub — click it any time and you get the newest version. It never redirects you: the fetch and download happen right here in the page.
